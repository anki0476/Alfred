# Alfred landing page

The interactive computer is adapted at the user's request from Ankit Shrivastava's `anki0476/creativejuices` portfolio, `src/RetroComputer.tsx` (Git blob `8cae387a9d6fcea6558a4e031e5e7f3868cc0c8f`). Source: https://github.com/anki0476/creativejuices/blob/main/src/RetroComputer.tsx

The surrounding design follows that portfolio's homepage composition. Copy, controls and responsive layout are adapted for Alfred.

The landing page is served at `/`, and all study entry points lead to `/studio`. The former `/welcome` preview route redirects to `/`. Landing and FAQ styles use the `alfred-` prefix; the studio retains its own styling and voice controls.
